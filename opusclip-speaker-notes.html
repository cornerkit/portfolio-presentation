Impact

Slide 3
Background
As creators increasingly film, edit, and publish on their phones, OpusClip needed a mobile experience to support their full workflow.

OpusClip's AI clip-generation engine only lived on the web. As more creators started filming and editing entirely from their phones, the product needed a mobile presence to meet them where they already were, without waiting to get back to a desktop.

Slide 4
Design Process
Simplifying the workflow, rethinking the interaction.

We scoped the mobile MVP around OpusClip's core value: helping creators turn long videos into short, publishable clips with as little friction as possible. Instead of reproducing the full desktop workflow, we identified which steps were essential to completing that job and which controls could be simplified or left out.

From there, the design focus is how each step should work on a phone, comparing interaction patterns to find the clearest and most efficient mobile experience without removing the moments of creator control that mattered.

Slide 5
Design Challenge 01 · Presets
HMW help mobile users select presets effortlessly?

Before AI could generate clips, users needed to configure clip length, language, genre, and style. The challenge was making setup easy to scan and complete on a small screen.

Slide 6
Challenge 01 — Iterations
Preset selection iterations
1st — All-in-one page

Comprehensive control, but too many options on one page causes information overload.

2nd — Step-by-step wizard

One choice at a time, but forces users through all options and makes backtracking hard.

3rd — One-page summary

Fast and lightweight, but editable controls split between the right side and bottom sheets.

Final — One summary, one bottom interaction zone

All editable controls consolidated into a single bottom zone — easier to review and adjust in one place.

Slide 7
Design Challenge 02 · Compare Clips
HMW help mobile users preview and compare AI-generated clips?

Once AI generated multiple clips, users needed enough context to compare alternatives and decide which to publish — on a small screen.

Slide 8
Challenge 02 — Iterations
Clip comparison iterations
1st — Click & scroll

Easy to scan, but switching between clips required returning to the list — comparison felt slow.

2nd — Swipe left & right

Multiple swipe directions made interactions hard to discover and conflicted with system back gestures.

Final — Vertical swipe, explicit controls for context

Fast clip comparison with vertical swiping. AI scores, reasoning, and transcripts surfaced through explicit controls.

Slide 9
Next Steps
Bring advanced editing tools to mobile

The launched version focuses on core flows. Next, we plan to bring advanced capabilities from the web — editing tools, caption customization, and more — to enable full on-the-go video creation.
